Model Sample Writing (2018 sky100)

Target Structures Spotted: 1. Not only..., but also (Inversion) 2. Participle Phrase 3. Provided that 4. It is + adj./p.p. + that 5. Should you have... (Inversion)

Dear Parents,

To facilitate learning outside the classroom and broaden students' horizons, I am writing to inform you of an upcoming school trip to Sky100, which all Class 6A students will attend next month. Not only will this excursion deepen their understanding of the city, but it will also provide an enriching and memorable experience.

Located on the 100th floor of the International Commerce Centre (ICC) at 1 Austin Road, West Kowloon, Sky100 Observation Deck offers a panoramic view of Hong Kong. Students are required to make their own way there and assemble at the main entrance of ICC by 10:00 a.m. on 5 May 2018. For those who plan to travel by public transport, taking the MTR to Kowloon Station or boarding Minibus Route 74 are both convenient options.

Regarding lunch arrangements, Sky100 has generously offered a special student rate of $30 per lunchbox. If your child wishes to purchase one, please remind them to bring enough cash on the day. Alternatively, students who prefer to dine at nearby restaurants are welcome to do so, provided that they return to Sky100 by 1:30 p.m.

In addition, please provide your child with a $150 cheque for the entrance fee. It is sincerely hoped that this outing will be a rewarding experience for everyone. Should you have any enquiries, please do not hesitate to email me at chriswong@mss.edu.hk or call me at 12345678.

Yours sincerely,

Chris Wong

Class Teacher of 6A

DSE Exam Secret: What MUST Be Included in the Conclusion?

In HKDSE Part A Parents' Letters, markers look for 4 essential closing elements to award top marks for Content and Tone/Register:

1. Goodwill & Expectation

Express sincere hope that the trip will benefit students (e.g., "It is sincerely hoped that this outing will be rewarding...").

2. Polite Enquiry Invitation

Use formal inversion to invite questions (e.g., "Should you have any enquiries, please do not hesitate to...").

3. Teacher Contact Info

Provide clear email address and school phone number so parents can reach you easily.

4. Formal Sign-Off (+ Reply Slip)

Close with "Yours sincerely," + Full Name & Title (Class Teacher of 6A). Remember parents' letters pair with a Reply Slip!
